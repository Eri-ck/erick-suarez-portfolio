Research Understanding people and their behavior before proposing any solution. I enjoy discovering why users act the way they do through interviews, usability testing, user journeys, and information architecture. By analyzing insights and identifying pain points, I can make informed design decisions that create intuitive, accessible, and meaningful experiences instead of relying on assumptions. I believe that strong research is the foundation of every successful product because it ensures that every design decision is backed by real user needs and business goals.
Systems Designing reusable foundations that create consistency and scalability across an entire product, not just individual screens. I enjoy building design systems with design tokens, reusable components, interaction states, and clear documentation that help teams work faster while maintaining a cohesive user experience. A well-crafted system improves collaboration between designers and developers, reduces inconsistencies, and makes future product growth more efficient and sustainable.
Product Understanding how user needs, business objectives, and technical constraints come together to shape successful digital products. I enjoy thinking beyond individual interfaces by considering the impact of every design decision on the overall product strategy. By balancing usability, feasibility, and business value, I aim to create experiences that solve real problems, support company goals, and deliver meaningful value for both users and stakeholders.
Engineering Bringing ideas closer to reality by understanding how designs are built and implemented. I enjoy exploring frontend development, creating interactive prototypes, and experimenting with modern technologies to better connect design and engineering. Thinking about implementation from the beginning helps me design experiences that are not only visually polished but also technically feasible, efficient to build, and consistent across different platforms and devices..
